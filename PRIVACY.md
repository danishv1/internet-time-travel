# Privacy Policy — Internet Time Travel

**Internet Time Travel does not collect, store remotely, or transmit any user data.**

- All settings (chosen date, on/off state, site exceptions) are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
- A small cache of public video upload dates is also stored locally, to avoid repeated lookups.
- The extension makes requests only to the sites you visit, to `web.archive.org` (to load archived snapshots), and to `youtube.com` (to read public video metadata). These requests carry no identifying information from the extension.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts.

If you uninstall the extension, all of its stored data is deleted by Chrome.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
